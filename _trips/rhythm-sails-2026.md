<div class="day-block">
  <h3>The trip</h3>
  <div class="day-sub">Rhythm &amp; Sails · St. Vincent and the Grenadines</div>
  <div class="entry">A week sailing the Grenadines as part of the Rhythm &amp; Sails music charter, sharing the boat and the islands with a run of artists including Greensky Bluegrass's Anders Beck.</div>
  <div class="entry">Five guest boats, eight days, four islands. Artists rotated between boats all week playing dinghy sessions off the sterns, and the shore nights were full concerts at Bushmans, Basil's Bar, the Plantation Hotel and Firefly.</div>
</div>

<div class="day-block" id="day1">
  <h3>Saturday, May 2 · Day One</h3>
  <div class="day-sub">Blue Lagoon Marina → Young Island Cut</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">2:45 PM</span> Guests arrive at Blue Lagoon Marina to a welcome song at the entrance, musicians set up and playing.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">3:30 PM</span> Load personal gear, meet your crew, boat walkthrough with the captain. First mates collect passports for the cruising permit.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">4:15 PM</span> Group safety and trip meeting on Ashley's boat.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">5:00 PM</span> Set sail from Blue Lagoon Marina for Young Island Cut.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">5:30 PM</span> Swim, snorkel, hike Fort Duvernette.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">6:30 PM</span> Dinner and drinks aboard.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">7:00 PM</span> Bryan playing sax from a dinghy off the aft of each guest boat.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">8:30 PM</span> Musicians work their way around all five guest boats for a few songs each.</div>
</div>

<div class="day-block" id="day2">
  <h3>Sunday, May 3 · Day Two</h3>
  <div class="day-sub">Young Island Cut → Salt Whistle Bay, Mayreau · 32 NM, 6–7 hrs</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">5:30 AM</span> Long sail south. Sleep in if you want, breakfast and snacks underway, lines out for fish.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">11:00 AM</span> Arrive Salt Whistle Bay, mooring balls first come first served.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">12:00 PM</span> Lunch on boats.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">1:00 PM</span> Beach hangs, boat hangs, snorkeling, bar hopping, floating docks, SUPs. The greatest afternoon imaginable, per the run of show.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">6:00 PM</span> Dinner on boats.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">8:00 PM</span> Concert at Bushmans, everyone ashore, bean bags and coolers hauled up the beach.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">10:00 PM</span> Back to the boats.</div>
  <div class="day-quote">"Choose joy today." <span class="who">Bartholomew Roberts, a.k.a. Black Bart</span></div>
</div>

<div class="day-block" id="day3">
  <h3>Monday, May 4 · Day Three</h3>
  <div class="day-sub">Mayreau layover · Windward Resort · Alter Ego Night at Black Boys</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">7:30 AM</span> Early breakfast for divers, pickup at 9:00.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">9:30 AM</span> Beach vibes, boat hangs, docks, SUPs, snorkeling, bumba shack hangs.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">1:30 PM</span> Divers back, lunch on boats. Headcount taken for tennis in Mustique.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">2:00 PM</span> Up to Windward Resort by beach walk or shuttle. Live music, pool swims, jams, cocktails.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">4:30 PM</span> Back to the boats to get dressed for Alter Ego Night.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">6:30 PM</span> BBQ at Black Boys, covered by Rhythm &amp; Sails, steel drum band playing.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">8:00 PM</span> Live music into a full reggae island dance party.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">10:00 PM</span> Back to the boats, skydeck hangs.</div>
  <div class="tip">
    <div class="lbl">Alter Ego Night</div>
    <p>Brendan and Jen went as Kenny Rogers and Dolly Parton. Full commitment, full wigs, presumably some Islands in the Stream karaoke.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="day-quote">"Keep calm and carry on." <span class="who">Snoop Dogg</span></div>
</div>

<div class="day-block" id="day4">
  <h3>Tuesday, May 5 · Day Four</h3>
  <div class="day-sub">Mayreau → Britannia Bay, Mustique · 19 NM, 3–4 hrs</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">7:00 AM</span> Breakfast on boats.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">8:00 AM</span> Depart Salt Whistle Bay for Mustique.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">11:00 AM</span> Arrive Britannia Bay, lunch aboard.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">1:00 PM</span> Ashore, homebase at Basil's Bar. Taxis and mules shuttle up to Cotton House Beach Bar, shopping in the gift stores while you wait.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">1:30 PM</span> Cotton House Beach Bar: swimming, snorkeling, drinks, lounge chairs, island tours running all afternoon.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">2:00 PM</span> Tennis.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">4:30 PM</span> Back to the boats to change and refresh.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">6:30 PM</span> Dinner on boats or at Basil's Bar.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">9:00 PM</span> Live music at Basil's Bar. Jon Stickley flew into Mustique that afternoon for it.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">11:00 PM</span> Back to the boats.</div>
  <div class="day-quote">"Live, laugh, love." <span class="who">John Rackham, a.k.a. Calico Jack</span></div>
</div>

<div class="day-block" id="day5">
  <h3>Wednesday, May 6 · Day Five</h3>
  <div class="day-sub">Mustique → Admiralty Bay, Bequia · 12 NM, 1.5–2 hrs</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">8:00 AM</span> Breakfast on boats.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">8:30 AM</span> Daniel Donato dinghies around to every boat to say goodbye before his flight out.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">9:00 AM</span> Depart Britannia Bay for Bequia.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">10:30 AM</span> Arrive Admiralty Bay, moorings close in to Mac's, Dive Bequia and the Plantation Hotel.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">12:30 PM</span> Lunch on boats.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">1:30 PM</span> Guest's choice: shopping, bars, explore, or stay aboard. Bar One, Mac's, Jack's, or walk the dinghy dock around to Princess Margaret Beach.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">Afternoon</span> Princess Margaret Beach hang: beach games, swimming, live music.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">Evening</span> Dinner on boats, then everyone up to the Plantation Hotel for live music. Drinks on personal tabs.</div>
  <div class="day-quote">"Nice sunburn." <span class="who">Johnny Depp, a.k.a. Jack Sparrow</span></div>
</div>

<div class="day-block" id="day6">
  <h3>Thursday, May 7 · Day Six</h3>
  <div class="day-sub">Bequia layover · The Anders Beck Golf Invitational at Firefly</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">7:30 AM</span> Early breakfast for divers, pickup at 8:00.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">8:30 AM</span> Breakfast on boats.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">9:30 AM</span> Guest's choice: boat hangs, beach hangs, shopping, bars, beaches. Whatever fills your cup.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">12:30 PM</span> Divers back, lunch on boats.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">3:00 PM</span> Fatman Taxi from the dinghy dock up to Firefly Bequia.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">4:00 PM</span> Buy drink tickets, settle in on the patio.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">4:30 PM</span> Welcome announcement, then the Anders Beck Golf Invitational. Pool, cocktails, questionable form.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">7:00 PM</span> Curry buffet dinner at Firefly, covered by Rhythm &amp; Sails.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">9:00 PM</span> Live music upstairs at Firefly.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">10:30 PM</span> Back to the boats for skydeck hangs. Final night.</div>
</div>
