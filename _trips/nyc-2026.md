  <div class="day-sub">Land 1:30 PM · hotel by 2:30 · Rudy's · Tailor at 5:00 · Goose at MSG</div>

  <dl class="facts">
    <div><dt>Arrive</dt><dd>1:30 PM, hotel by ~2:30 PM</dd></div>
    <div><dt>Hotel</dt><dd>Aloft New York Chelsea, 132 W 28th St</dd></div>
    <div><dt>Dinner</dt><dd>The Tailor Public House, 505 8th Ave · 5:00 PM reservation</dd></div>
    <div><dt>Show</dt><dd>Goose Night One · Madison Square Garden</dd></div>
  </dl>

  <div class="section-rule"><span class="eyebrow">The afternoon</span></div>

  <div class="stop">
    <div class="num"><b>1</b><span class="at">2:30 PM</span></div>
    <div class="body">
      <h4>Check in, Aloft New York Chelsea</h4>
      <div class="addr">132 W 28th St</div>
      <p>Drop the bags, get your bearings. A couple hours before dinner: one stop, one cold beer, and a free hot dog before you walk to Tailor.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="stop">
    <div class="num"><b>2</b><span class="at">3:30 PM</span></div>
    <div class="body">
      <h4>Rudy's Bar &amp; Grill</h4>
      <div class="addr">627 9th Ave · 10 min walk northwest</div>
      <p>A Hell's Kitchen institution pouring cheap beer since 1933. Dark, loud, full of regulars, and they hand you a free hot dog with every beer. No pretense. Exactly what a snack and a beer should look like in New York. One or two rounds and you're perfectly set for dinner at 5.</p>
      <div class="tip">
        <div class="lbl">Local tip</div>
        <p>Cash only. Free hot dogs with every drink, just ask for one.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="section-rule"><span class="eyebrow">Tonight</span></div>

  <div class="tonight">
    <div class="slot">
      <div class="lbl">Dinner</div>
      <h4>The Tailor Public House</h4>
      <div class="addr">505 8th Ave · across from MSG · 5:00 PM reservation</div>
      <p>Irish pub with real energy, live music Fridays, great Guinness. Get there early, walk across the street when it's time.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="slot show">
      <div class="lbl">The show</div>
      <h4>Goose, Night One</h4>
      <div class="addr">Madison Square Garden, 4 Penn Plaza</div>
      <p>First of two nights at the Garden. Full production, sold-out crowd. Let it unfold.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="section-rule"><span class="eyebrow">While you're in the neighborhood</span></div>

  <div class="recs">
    <div class="rec">
      <span class="cat">Coffee</span>
      <h4>Sullivan Street Bakery</h4>
      <div class="addr">533 W 47th St · near Craft+Carry</div>
      <p>Rustic Italian bakery. Bomboloni, artisan bread, cold brew. Best in Hell's Kitchen.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="rec">
      <span class="cat">Coffee</span>
      <h4>Kobrick Coffee Co.</h4>
      <div class="addr">24 9th Ave · near Chelsea Market</div>
      <p>Family-run since 1920. Hand-roasts in small batches. Old school NYC. No scene, just great coffee.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="rec">
      <span class="cat">Pizza</span>
      <h4>Don Antonio</h4>
      <div class="addr">309 W 50th St · Hell's Kitchen</div>
      <p>Wood-fired Neapolitan. Truffle burrata pie is serious. Locals eat here.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="rec">
      <span class="cat">Pizza</span>
      <h4>Upside Pizza</h4>
      <div class="addr">598 8th Ave · near MSG</div>
      <p>Time Out top-12 NYC. The Falcowitz (white wine cream, mushroom) is the one.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="rec">
      <span class="cat">Eats</span>
      <h4>Coppelia</h4>
      <div class="addr">207 W 14th St · after Chelsea Market</div>
      <p>24hr Latin diner. Cuban sandwich, empanadas, great coffee. Fast, cheap, zero wait.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="rec">
      <span class="cat">Eats</span>
      <h4>Westville Hudson</h4>
      <div class="addr">333 Hudson St · off the High Line</div>
      <p>Vegetable-forward plates and sandwiches. Good for grazing, low key, quick.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="rec">
      <span class="cat">Beer</span>
      <h4>9th Ave Saloon</h4>
      <div class="addr">656 9th Ave · Hell's Kitchen</div>
      <p>Dive bar. Cash only, cheap beers, zero attitude. Pool table, jukebox. Opens at noon.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="rec">
      <span class="cat">Beer</span>
      <h4>Frying Pan</h4>
      <div class="addr">Pier 66, W 26th St · Hudson River</div>
      <p>Floating bar on a retired lightship. Outdoor deck, cold beer, Hudson skyline. Unmissable afternoon stop.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="section-rule"><span class="eyebrow">The morning route, stop by stop</span></div>

  <div class="stop">
    <div class="num"><b>1</b><span class="at">8:05 AM</span></div>
    <div class="body">
      <h4>787 Coffee Co.</h4>
      <div class="addr">251 W 30th St · 3 min walk north</div>
      <p>Puerto Rican owned, roast their own beans, you pick the variety. Order the coquito latte or dulce de leche. Not a chain, not a scene, exceptional coffee made by people who care.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="stop">
    <div class="num"><b>2</b><span class="at">8:30 AM</span></div>
    <div class="body">
      <h4>The High Line, 30th St entrance</h4>
      <div class="addr">W 30th St &amp; 10th Ave · 10 min walk west</div>
      <p>Walk west on 30th to the 10th Ave entrance and head south. Morning is the move: light crowds, soft light, city waking up below you. 1.45 miles through Chelsea on a decommissioned freight rail. Take your time.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="stop">
    <div class="num"><b>3</b><span class="at">9:30 AM</span></div>
    <div class="body">
      <h4>Chelsea Market</h4>
      <div class="addr">75 9th Ave · the High Line exits here</div>
      <p>Built inside the old Nabisco factory, runs a full city block. Still calm at 9:30, locals doing their rounds before the crowds arrive. Los Tacos No. 1 is the move: adobada on a fresh tortilla.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="stop">
    <div class="num"><b>4</b><span class="at">10:30 AM</span></div>
    <div class="body">
      <h4>The Grey Dog, Chelsea</h4>
      <div class="addr">242 W 16th St · 4 min walk south</div>
      <p>Chelsea's neighborhood brunch spot. No pretense, no scene. Warm room, honest food. Breakfast sandwich is excellent. Burger is legit.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="stop">
    <div class="num"><b>5</b><span class="at">12:00 PM</span></div>
    <div class="body">
      <h4>Craft+Carry Hell's Kitchen</h4>
      <div class="addr">751 9th Ave · walk north</div>
      <p>24 rotating craft taps, board games, zero attitude. Opens at noon. Tell them what you like and they'll steer you right. Perfect pre-show wind-down.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
