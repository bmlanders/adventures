<div class="day-block">
  <h3>The trip</h3>
  <div class="day-sub">Three cities, two day trips, one very long list of custard tarts</div>

  <dl class="facts">
    <div><dt>Arrive</dt><dd>Lisbon, Jan 23 at 8:10 AM</dd></div>
    <div><dt>Lisbon</dt><dd>Eurostars Lisboa Baixa · Jan 23–27, 4 nights</dd></div>
    <div><dt>Aveiro</dt><dd>1877 Estrela Palace Hotel · Jan 27–29, 2 nights</dd></div>
    <div><dt>Porto</dt><dd>Exmo Hotel by Olivia · Jan 29 – Feb 1, 3 nights</dd></div>
    <div><dt>Depart</dt><dd>Porto, Feb 1 at 6:00 AM</dd></div>
  </dl>
</div>

<div class="day-block" id="jan23">
  <h3>Thursday, Jan 23 · Lisbon</h3>
  <div class="day-sub">Land, drop the bags, eat immediately</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">8:10 AM</span> Land at Lisbon Airport, Uber or Bolt into the city.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">9:30 AM</span> Check in at the Eurostars Lisboa Baixa, or at least leave the luggage.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">10:30 AM</span> Straight onto a Lisbon food tour, three to four hours of working through Portuguese delicacies.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">3:00 PM</span>
    <div class="entry-body">Ease off for the afternoon. Wander the Baixa and the river promenade, and climb the Rua Augusta Arch for a first look over the city.
      <div class="places"><span>Lisbon downtown</span><span>River promenade</span><span>Rua Augusta Arch</span></div>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">7:30 PM</span> Dinner near the hotel, then sleep off the flight.</div>
</div>

<div class="day-block" id="jan24">
  <h3>Friday, Jan 24 · Alfama and Fado</h3>
  <div class="day-sub">Miradouros all morning, Fado house at night</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">9:00 AM</span>
    <div class="entry-body">Alfama from the top down. Take the free Castelo elevator up, then a second one inside the Pingo Doce supermarket, and walk down through the viewpoints.
      <div class="places"><span>Miradouro do Chão do Loureiro</span><span>S. Jorge Castle</span><span>Miradouro do Recolhimento</span><span>Portas do Sol</span><span>Santa Luzia</span><span>Sé de Lisboa</span></div>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">12:30 PM</span> Lunch in Alfama.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">3:00 PM</span>
    <div class="entry-body">Downtown on foot.
      <div class="places"><span>Praça do Comércio</span><span>Rossio</span><span>Santa Justa Elevator</span><span>Bertrand Chiado</span><span>Camões Square</span><span>Cais do Sodré</span><span>Ribeira das Naus</span></div>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">8:00 PM</span> Dinner at a Fado house. Sr. Vinho, O Faia, or Parreirinha de Alfama.</div>
  <div class="tip">
    <div class="lbl">Worth knowing</div>
    <p>Fado is UNESCO-listed Intangible Heritage. You eat while they sing, and it is the one thing in Lisbon you genuinely cannot get anywhere else.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="day-block" id="jan25">
  <h3>Saturday, Jan 25 · Évora</h3>
  <div class="day-sub">Day trip by train · Roman temple, bone chapel, and a spa in a Roman bath</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">10:06 AM</span> IC train to Évora out of Entrecampos.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">11:35 AM</span>
    <div class="entry-body">Arrive and start walking.
      <div class="places"><span>Public Garden</span><span>San Francisco Church</span><span>Chapel of Bones</span></div>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">1:00 PM</span> Lunch at Tua Madre, regional flavours crossed with Italian cooking. Or Taberna Típica Quarta-Feira, where the chef picks and you find out when it arrives.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">2:30 PM</span>
    <div class="entry-body">More of the old town. Évora's cathedral is the largest medieval one in Portugal and the terrace views are the reason to go up.
      <div class="places"><span>Giraldo Square</span><span>Évora Cathedral</span><span>Páteo de São Miguel</span><span>Roman Temple</span><span>Diana Garden viewpoint</span></div>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">4:30 PM</span> The one big choice of the day: exclusive-use Roman baths at Acqua Veritas, or a visit and tasting at Adega da Cartuxa just outside town.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">7:06 PM</span> Train back, into Lisbon around 8:30.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">9:00 PM</span> Late dinner near the hotel.</div>
</div>

<div class="day-block" id="jan26">
  <h3>Sunday, Jan 26 · Cascais and Belém</h3>
  <div class="day-sub">Coast in the morning, monuments and custard tarts in the afternoon</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">9:00 AM</span> Uber out to Cascais, 30 to 40 minutes down the coast.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">10:00 AM</span>
    <div class="entry-body">Get dropped at Boca do Inferno and walk back toward town, which puts the promenade in front of you the whole way.
      <div class="places"><span>Boca do Inferno</span><span>Santa Maria Lighthouse</span><span>Cascais Citadel</span><span>Cascais Marina</span><span>Town center</span></div>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">12:30 PM</span> Lunch at Hífen, Mar do Inferno, or Baía do Peixe.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">3:30 PM</span> On to Belém.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">4:00 PM</span>
    <div class="entry-body">Belém from the outside, saving the interiors for the sightseeing.
      <div class="places"><span>MAAT</span><span>Mosteiro dos Jerónimos</span><span>Padrão dos Descobrimentos</span><span>Torre de Belém</span></div>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="tip">
    <div class="lbl">The actual mission</div>
    <p>Pastéis de Belém and Manteigaria are the two contenders for the best pastel de nata in the country. Get one of each and settle it yourself. The lines are long and worth it.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">7:30 PM</span> Back to the centre for dinner.</div>
</div>

<div class="day-block" id="jan27">
  <h3>Monday, Jan 27 · North to Aveiro</h3>
  <div class="day-sub">Train up the coast · moliceiros, ovos moles, tripas</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">9:30 AM</span> IC train to Aveiro from Santa Apolónia.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">12:02 PM</span> Arrive Aveiro, check in at the 1877 Estrela Palace.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">1:00 PM</span> Lunch at Subenshi Sushi, the original location and reportedly the best in the country. Or Tasquinha da Ria, O Bairro, O Batel.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">3:00 PM</span>
    <div class="entry-body">A deliberately slow afternoon. Ride a moliceiro, the painted canal boat, then work through the local sweets: ovos moles from Peixinho or M1882, and tripas de Aveiro at Tripas da Praça.
      <div class="places"><span>Moliceiro ride</span><span>Ovos moles</span><span>Tripas de Aveiro</span></div>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">8:00 PM</span> Dinner in town.</div>
</div>

<div class="day-block" id="jan28">
  <h3>Tuesday, Jan 28 · Aveiro and Costa Nova</h3>
  <div class="day-sub">Parks and canals, then the striped houses on the beach</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">9:00 AM</span>
    <div class="entry-body">An unhurried morning around the city.
      <div class="places"><span>São Gonçalinho Chapel</span><span>S. António Park</span><span>Infante Dom Pedro Park</span><span>Aveiro Cathedral</span><span>Ribbon bridge</span></div>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">1:00 PM</span> Lunch back at one of yesterday's places.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">3:00 PM</span> Fifteen minutes out to Costa Nova for the famous striped houses and the Atlantic.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">6:00 PM</span> Back to the centre, and dinner.</div>
</div>

<div class="day-block" id="jan29">
  <h3>Wednesday, Jan 29 · Porto</h3>
  <div class="day-sub">Third city, second food tour, sunset over the river</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">9:15 AM</span> AP train to Porto Campanhã, just over half an hour.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">9:48 AM</span> Arrive Porto, check in at the Exmo Hotel by Olivia.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">10:30 AM</span>
    <div class="entry-body">First pass at the centre. No need to finish it, there's another day at the end for whatever's left.
      <div class="places"><span>Bolsa Palace</span><span>Sé do Porto</span><span>São Bento Station</span><span>Aliados Avenue</span><span>Carmo Church</span><span>Clérigos Tower</span><span>Cordoaria Gardens</span></div>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">2:00 PM</span> A Porto food tour, another three to four hours of it.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">6:00 PM</span> Sunset over the river from Morro Gardens, across in Gaia.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">8:00 PM</span> Dinner in Porto.</div>
</div>

<div class="day-block" id="jan30">
  <h3>Thursday, Jan 30 · Douro Valley</h3>
  <div class="day-sub">All day out in the wine country</div>
  <div class="entry">A full day trip up the Douro: two wineries, a long lunch, tastings, and a stretch on the river in a traditional boat. The valley is the oldest demarcated wine region in the world, and the terraces going down to the water are the whole point.</div>
</div>

<div class="day-block" id="jan31">
  <h3>Friday, Jan 31 · Last day in Porto</h3>
  <div class="day-sub">Market, gardens, and port where it's actually made</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">9:00 AM</span> Mercado do Bolhão. It's uphill on foot, about 25 minutes.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">11:00 AM</span> Crystal Palace Gardens for the views, a flat half-hour walk from the market.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">12:30 PM</span> Lunch, then no plan at all: shopping, strolling, or anything missed on the first day. A river cruise if the timing works.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">5:00 PM</span> Port tasting across the river at Graham's or Caves Calém.</div>
  <div class="entry"><span class="t">8:00 PM</span> Last dinner.</div>
</div>

<div class="day-block" id="feb1">
  <h3>Saturday, Feb 1 · Home</h3>
  <div class="day-sub">6:00 AM out of Porto</div>
  <div class="entry">Very early flight. Ten days, three cities, two food tours, and an unresolved argument about Belém versus Manteigaria.</div>
</div>
