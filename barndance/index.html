Where to sleep

Crash here, camp, or grab a room

Between my place and my buddy Sully's across the lake, we've got a lot of beds. I can't promise one for everyone, so the earlier you RSVP, the better your odds. Ask on the form and I'll see what I can do.

  • Camp on the property. Two acres, so bring a tent or a camper and spread out. Power, water, real bathrooms, and fire pits are all there.
  • Hotels, 5 to 15 minutes out: Comfort Inn & Suites, Peach Tree Inn & Suites, Oak Cove Resort, and Baymont by Wyndham.
  • Airbnb. The Sister Lakes area is about 15 minutes out, and rentals run cheap once fall hits.
